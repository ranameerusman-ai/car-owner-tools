Introduction

Different countries measure fuel economy in different units. The US uses miles per gallon, most of Europe uses litres per 100 km, and the UK uses miles per gallon too — but a UK gallon is bigger than a US gallon. Comparing cars across borders is easy once you convert first — this calculator does it in one step.

This calculator converts between them all — MPG (US), MPG (UK), L/100km and km/L — and works out the cost of a trip too. Enter a trip in miles or kilometres, gallons or litres, and see the answer in every unit at once.

How the units convert

All four units describe the same thing, so they convert exactly.

L/100km = litres used ÷ kilometres driven × 100. MPG (US) = 235.214 ÷ L/100km. MPG (UK) = MPG (US) × 1.201. km/L = 100 ÷ L/100km. For example, 30 MPG (US) is about 7.8 L/100km.

What do the numbers mean?

Different countries measure the same thing in different ways — this guide makes comparing cars across borders simple.

USA

MPG — higher is better

Miles per gallon: how far you go on one gallon. A bigger number means a more efficient car.

EUROPE

L/100km — lower is better

Litres per 100 kilometres: how much fuel you use for a fixed distance. A smaller number means a more efficient car.

UK GALLONS

UK vs US gallons

A UK gallon is about 20% bigger than a US gallon, so the same car shows a higher MPG number in the UK. Always check which gallon a figure uses.

GUIDE

What is good?

30 MPG (US) is about 7.8 L/100km. 40 MPG is about 5.9 L/100km. Hybrids often beat 50 MPG, or under 4.7 L/100km.

CITY VS MOTORWAY

Look beyond one number

City driving uses far more fuel than motorway cruising. Compare figures measured the same way.

WHY IT MATTERS

Fuel cost follows it

A car at 20 MPG costs twice as much in fuel per mile as one at 40 MPG. Small differences add up to hundreds of dollars a year.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert MPG to L/100km?

Divide 235.214 by the MPG figure. For example, 30 MPG (US) is 235.214 ÷ 30 = 7.8 L/100km. This calculator does it for you.

Why is UK MPG higher than US MPG for the same car?

Because a UK (imperial) gallon is 4.546 litres while a US gallon is 3.785 litres — about 20% bigger. The same distance on the same fuel gives a UK MPG figure about 1.2 times the US one.

What is a good L/100km?

Under 6 L/100km is good for a petrol car, under 5 is very good, and hybrids often manage under 4.5. Large SUVs and trucks are usually 10 L/100km or more.

Can I use this for diesel or electric cars?

Yes for diesel — the maths is the same. For electric cars the equivalent is kWh per 100 km, which is a different unit, so use the trip figures only as a rough comparison.

How do I convert km/L to MPG?

Multiply km/L by 2.352 to get US MPG. For example, 12.75 km/L is about 30 MPG (US). This calculator does it for you automatically.
