Introduction

Car insurance is one of the biggest yearly costs of owning a car, and the price can vary by thousands of dollars for the same driver — it depends on your state, your age, your car and your driving record.

This calculator gives you an estimate of what you might pay, before you buy a car or renew a policy. It uses published US average rates and typical adjustment factors. Just a few details about you and your car — your name, email, phone number and ZIP code stay private. The result is an estimate for comparison, to guide your decisions.

Five ways to save more

The estimate is a starting point — here is what saves you the most.

CLEAN RECORD

Drive carefully

A clean record is the best discount there is. It keeps your premium at its best — and the longer you keep it clean, the more you save.

COMPARE QUOTES

Shop around every year

Prices for the same cover vary a lot between insurers. Compare quotes from several companies before you renew.

RAISE YOUR DEDUCTIBLE

Pay more if you claim

A higher deductible usually means a lower monthly premium — as long as you can afford to pay it if you claim.

ASK ABOUT DISCOUNTS

Low mileage and safe driver

Ask about low-mileage, safe-driver and bundling discounts — asking is how you get them.

BUNDLE POLICIES

Home and auto together

Buying home and auto cover from the same insurer can save you money on both.

What affects your rate most?

Seven factors, in roughly the order insurers weigh them.

UP TO +85%

Driving record

A clean record is the best discount there is — it keeps your premium at its best.

UP TO +60%

Age

Drivers under 25 have the highest premiums, because insurers see more claims from young drivers. Rates usually improve a lot after 25.

STATE VARIES 2×

Where you live

Full coverage in Michigan can cost roughly twice as much as in Vermont, driven by local repair costs, theft and claim rates.

FULL VS LIABILITY

Coverage level

Full coverage costs roughly three times liability-only cover, because it also covers repairs to your own car.

UP TO +25%

Car value

A higher-value car costs more to repair or replace, so it costs more to insure. Older cars with a lower value cost less to cover.

UP TO +10%

Annual miles

More miles means more time on the road. Low-mileage drivers often pay less.

Frequently asked questions

Can I calculate car insurance with full privacy?

Yes — this calculator only needs your state, age group, car details, coverage and driving record. Your name, email, phone number and ZIP code stay private.

Is this a real insurance quote?

It is an estimate built from published US average rates and typical adjustment factors — a helpful guide before you ask an insurer for a real quote. A real quote will also use your ZIP code, exact car model and credit history, so it can be higher or lower.

Why do young drivers pay more for insurance?

Insurers price on claim statistics, and drivers under 25 make more claims per mile than older drivers. The extra amount usually shrinks a lot at 25 and keeps shrinking with a clean record.

What is the difference between liability and full coverage?

Liability only pays for repairs and injuries you cause to other people. Full coverage adds collision (repairs to your own car) and comprehensive (theft, fire, weather and similar events).

How can I lower my premium?

Keep a clean record, compare quotes from several insurers, raise your deductible, ask about low-mileage and safe-driver discounts, and bundle home and auto cover if you can.
