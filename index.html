Frequently asked questions

Are these calculators really free?

Yes. Every tool on this site is free to use, with unlimited use and instant access at every step.

Do you collect my data?

Your data stays with you. All calculations happen in your browser, and everything you enter stays on your device. See our Privacy Policy.

Are the results exact?

They are estimates based on standard formulas and, for insurance, published average rates. Your real-world numbers can vary — for example, actual insurance premiums also depend on your ZIP code and insurer. Treat results as a helpful guide for your decisions.

Do the tools work on my phone?

Yes. The whole site is built to work on phones, tablets and desktops, ready to use right in your browser.
