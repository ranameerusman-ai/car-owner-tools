About — Car Owner Tools

Car Owner Tools is a small site with one job: answer the questions every car owner asks, with a calculator that gives the answer in seconds.

We build free tools for tire sizes, fuel economy, trip costs, insurance estimates and car value. Every tool runs entirely in your browser — everything you type stays on your device. Open a tool and start right away — your details stay private.

How the tools work

Each calculator uses standard formulas and, for insurance, published US average rates with typical adjustment factors. Results are estimates to guide you — the maths is shown on the page so you can check it yourself. Where a figure depends on your exact car or location, we say so plainly.

Why we built it

Most car tools on the internet ask for personal details before giving an answer. We wanted tools that respect the person using them: fast, free, honest about being estimates, and written in plain English.

We also write short guides on the blog — how to work out your MPG, what changes your fuel costs, and how insurance estimates work — written in the same plain English as the tools. The site is free to use, with no account and no sign-up, and we keep adding new tools and guides over time.
