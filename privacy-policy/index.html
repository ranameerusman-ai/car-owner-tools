Privacy Policy — Car Owner Tools

Your information stays yours. All calculations on this site happen in your browser, on your device. Everything you type into a calculator stays on your device.

Last updated: 20 November 2026.

Information we collect

Your details stay private. The calculators work with instant access and full privacy — your name, email address, phone number, location and ZIP code stay with you.

Cookies and analytics

This site is tracking-cookie free — your browsing stays private. If analytics or advertising is added in future, this page will be updated first to describe exactly what it collects.

Third-party services

Pages load the Archivo and IBM Plex Mono fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google’s servers to fetch them. That is the only third-party request the site makes — everything else runs right here, on this site.

Contact

If you email us, we will use your message only to reply to you, and your message stays private. See our Contact page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new “last updated” date.
